About us

Networking without the friction

ScanByNexlink replaces paper business cards with a digital profile card that's always up to date, shareable via QR code or NFC tap, and viewable by anyone — no app install required.

The Problem We Solve

Sharing contact info at events and meetings is still awkward — reading out phone numbers, spelling handles, making people type your LinkedIn URL. Paper cards are static, costly, and easy to lose.

How It Works

Create once, share forever

Sign up, add your name, phone, LinkedIn, and any social links you want. We generate a unique QR code and a shareable link instantly.

QR + NFC

Show your QR code to anyone — they scan with their phone camera and see your info instantly in their browser. On mobile, you can also write your link to a physical NFC tag.

Always current

Change your number or add a new link anytime. Every QR code and NFC tag you've already shared updates automatically — no reprinting needed.

Private by design

Only what you choose to share is public. We never sell data, and our view stats are anonymous counts only — we never track who scanned your card.

Our Vision

We believe networking should be effortless. One profile, always up to date, accessible by anyone with a phone. No app install barriers, no outdated paper cards, no friction.

ScanByNexlink is built for professionals at conferences, freelancers sharing their portfolio, creators linking their platforms, and anyone who wants a faster way to connect.

Tech Stack

Built with a modern, lightweight stack designed for reliability and speed:

  • Web: Static frontend (migrating to Next.js for SSR)
  • Mobile: React Native via Expo — one codebase for iOS and Android
  • Backend: Supabase (Postgres + Auth + API)
  • Hosting: Vercel (web) + Expo EAS (mobile builds)
