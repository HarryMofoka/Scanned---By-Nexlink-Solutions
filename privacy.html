Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

1. Information We Collect

Account Information

When you create an account, we collect your email address and password (securely hashed — we never store your plain-text password). If you sign in with Google or Apple, we receive your name and email from the provider.

Profile Information

You choose what to share on your public profile: name, phone number, LinkedIn, and other social links. Only the fields you explicitly fill in are ever made public — everything else stays private.

View Statistics

We count views of your public profile to show you basic stats. We do not record who viewed it — stats are anonymous counts only, with no IP addresses, device fingerprints, or viewer identity stored.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To create and maintain your account
  • To display your public profile when someone scans your QR code or taps your NFC tag
  • To show you anonymous view statistics for your own profile
  • To send account-related emails (password reset, security notices)

3. What We Share

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal data with third parties. Your public profile information is visible to anyone who scans your QR code or visits your profile link — this is by design and under your control.

4. Data Storage & Security

Your data is stored in a managed Postgres database (Supabase) with encryption at rest. Authentication is handled by Supabase Auth — we never touch your password directly. All connections use HTTPS.

5. Your Rights

You can:

  • Edit or remove any information from your profile at any time
  • Delete your account entirely — this removes all your data permanently
  • Request a copy of your stored data by contacting support

We comply with POPIA (South Africa) and GDPR (EU) requirements where applicable.

6. Cookies & Tracking

We use essential session cookies for authentication. We do not use advertising cookies, analytics trackers, or third-party tracking pixels.

7. Data Retention

Account data is retained as long as your account is active. Abuse-prevention logs (IP addresses, timestamps) are kept for 30–90 days and then deleted. View statistics are retained as aggregate counts only.

8. Contact

Questions about this policy? Reach out at privacy@scanbynexlink.com
